    What counts on the bell, and what stays quiet

    • Red slot, counted: a step stopped (the CV can’t be read, nobody could be assigned). It turns quiet once someone retries or moves the candidate.
    • Magenta slot, counted: an interviewer passed and the step is now short of people; an offer accepted or declined; a hire. For interviewers: a new task for them, a time booked or called off.
    • Grey slot, listed only: scorecards sent, interviews booked, documents in, moves by other managers, new applications (one line per job per day).
    • Never shown: your own moves; what a move causes by itself (the interview it cancels, the offer it withdraws); claims, reminders, re-tries; rejections the flow made on its own.
    • Opening the tray clears the number. A click opens the candidate at the right place (the step, the offer). × hides one line; Clear all hides all of them.
