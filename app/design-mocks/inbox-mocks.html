Inbox: take a task, see your week, look back

Mock for approval, no code changed yet. Seen as Maya Reyes, a Shift lead (employee), on Monday 26 October. The last tab shows a manager. In the first tab, try Take it and Not for me.

Tasks: one list, two groups

  • Up for grabs holds tasks the workflow sent to a role or to several people. The first person to take one gets it, and it leaves everyone else’s list. A task sent only to you skips this group and goes straight to Yours.
  • The detail shows your next 7 days beside the choice, so you can see if you have room before you take it.
  • Not for me hides it from you only. If everyone passes, it goes to the manager’s “Needs attention” list as Blocked, as it does now.
  • The empty outline avatar means nobody holds it yet. When you take it, the slot on the card turns magenta, as it does for every task a person holds.

Everyone’s schedule: the team’s week as a calendar

  • Managers get a Mine · Everyone switch in Schedule. Everyone shows the whole team’s booked interviews and trial shifts on a week calendar.
  • The left column becomes the team list. Tick people to show or hide them. The count says how many interviews each person has this week, so you can see who is busy before you assign or reassign.
  • Every event shows its interviewer’s initials. They all use the one accent colour: in Lanyard, colour means status, not a person. Past events turn grey.
  • Choosing an event opens a small card with Open task and Open candidate. Weekends are narrow because most shops interview Monday to Friday. They widen when something is booked.
