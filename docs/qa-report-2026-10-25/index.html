Verdict

The core loop works end to end with real people, real CVs and real AI: post a job, take applications, AI screen, assign reviewers, candidate self-books a panel interview, blind scorecards, offer, accept. Permissions, validation and failure handling are solid, and the candidate page is the strongest screen in the app. Three things need fixing before an SMB launch: the built-in flow templates mark people Hired without an offer, the AI flow builder can produce a flow that has nothing to read, and the Gemini key is on a free tier that runs out after about 20 AI calls a day.

Read this first

Top issues

  1. Templates hire people without an offer High

    All three hire templates end interview → decision → send "Offer email" → close as Hired. Marcus Tan was marked Hired the moment the second scorecard landed, with no letter, terms or acceptance. The Offer feature (letters, accept page) only works if a manager steps in before the interview finishes, and the job ended at 2 hires for 1 seat.

  2. AI flow builder skips the CV it was told to read High

    "The AI reads their CV and checks…" produced a flow with no CV field and no Read CV step, and the banner said No problems found. The builder only wires fields that already exist on the form.

  3. Gemini free tier caps the whole AI layer High

    Limit 20 requests per day. After ~20 calls, Ask, the flow builder and AI review summaries all fail. The user sees "The AI service did not reply". A paid key is a launch requirement.

  4. Reviewers work blind in their own task pane Medium

    "Call Ravi Krishnan" shows no phone number. "Review Nurul (score 67)" shows no scores, summary or CV. Every task means a round trip to the candidate page.

  5. Links and decisions never reach the candidate Medium

    Booking and offer links are copied by hand, manual rejects send nothing, and an accepted offer silently orphans a booked interview.

  6. Offer signing rejects common Singapore names Medium

    "Nurul Binte Hidayah" was refused because the check wants first + last name exactly. Malay, Indian and surname-first Chinese names will hit this.

What worked
  • Ranking matched the expected order on every job (11 of 13 routed as a recruiter would).
  • Summaries cite evidence a manager can check: years, named certifications, "forklift course in progress until Dec 2026".
  • Hidden prompt-injection text had no effect.
  • Identity is redacted before scoring; the "Why" card shows each rule the score passed or failed.
What to tune
  • Must-have chips are literal. "SAP EWM" cost Nurul 42 points for Manhattan WMS, though the JD says "or similar". Let a chip hold alternatives.
  • Rules written only in the description (demerit points) are invisible to the AI.
  • Skill extraction fragments words, so conditions on "Skills" are unreliable today.
  • Every summary spends one Gemini call; budget for applicants × steps per day.
Effectiveness and usefulness

Does it click into place?

Premise: users will be trained on building and customizing flows, so nothing is proposed for removal just because a newcomer wouldn't find it. The bar is that each part is easy to learn, intuitive once learned, and connected to the parts around it. Most recommendations are therefore simplify, rename, default better or connect better.

Simplify
  • Replace the template ending with a Make offer person step that completes when the offer is accepted or declined.
  • Add a "Rating 1–5" answer type; today it's Number + Lowest + Highest every time.
  • Hide Key fields under an Advanced toggle and derive them silently.
  • When Booking is switched on, list panelists missing hours and offer to nudge them.
  • Default time zone and currency from company settings, not the browser.
Remove or merge
  • Merge "Offer email" send step into the Offer feature; two things called "offer" with different behaviour confuse even trained users.
  • Hide engine-only fields (File checksum, CV text) from condition pickers; nothing a manager would branch on.
  • Nothing else qualified for removal: every feature tested had a real use in at least one of the three jobs.
Learnability
  • Strong: plain-language copy everywhere ("Why", "This will…", "Fix 1 thing before publishing"), a "Your board" preview of columns, live rule summaries.
  • Weak: the same idea has two names (Stages/Steps, Decision/Screen, Person step/Task), which training material will have to map. Partly: Words in Help maps them
  • AI builder entry is hidden in the canvas toolbar and the blank-job screen, not in the New job dialog. Addressed: wizard step 2
Missing or new flows
  • Send booking link, offer link, rejection and cancellation emails from the app.
  • Seat-aware hiring: warn at the last seat, offer to close the job.
  • Hires list with names and start dates for onboarding.
  • Decline reason on the offer page; "send rejection email" option on manual reject.
  • Closed-job page links to other open roles.

  • Manager: Jithin (job owner for all jobs).

Data

  • 12 CVs (9 PDF, 3 DOCX) plus scanned, fake and 12 MB edge files.
  • 4 offers, 2 bookings, 3 scorecards, 1 overdue reminder.
Caveats
  • Neon DB is in us-east-2; every API call took 3.6–6 s from this laptop. Speed is not representative of production.
  • Email is mocked; content of flow emails could not be checked, only that they fired once.
  • Ask and AI builder stress prompts are untested beyond the quota error.
